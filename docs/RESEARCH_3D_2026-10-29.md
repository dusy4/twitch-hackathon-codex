# Characters with depth: research and implementation decision

Research date: 2026-10-29. Owner requested deep research before implementation after rejecting the flat appearance. No renderer implementation was changed during collection. Exa searches requested 54 result slots across seven searches; these are candidate slots, not 54 independently validated sources. Firecrawl also searched two eight-result branches. Known URLs were subsequently retrieved through Exa and live Firecrawl scraping. Duplicate repository/page results were merged conceptually below. This covers material alternatives, not every library on the internet.

## Diagnosis

The existing renderer uses BoxGeometry but fixes an orthographic camera straight ahead. Avatar positions map directly to image x/y, with almost constant z. It provides no ground-plane perspective, orbit/parallax, skeletal asset animation, or observed camera pose. The user's visual criticism is valid: more primitive boxes and placement work do not solve this. Server identity, entrances, chat commands and persistent economy can remain behind a new rendering adapter.

Three separate capabilities must be verified: volumetric character geometry/animation; perspective virtual-world depth/contact; and real-camera pose/depth. A library satisfying one does not prove the other two.

## Engines and reusable systems

| Solution | Evidence and integration implications | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing Three.js + GLTFLoader + AnimationMixer + SkeletonUtils | Official cloning creates independent bones while sharing geometry/materials; mixer controls independent action playback/crossfades. [SkeletonUtils](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/module-SkeletonUtils.html), [AnimationMixer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/AnimationMixer.html) | Select. Use actual skinned assets, independent mixers, a PerspectiveCamera and ground-ray projection. A wholesale engine migration is unnecessary to achieve depth. |
| Babylon.js | Apache-2.0 full engine; official animation retargeting includes bone maps, rest-pose correction and root/ground correction. [Repository](https://github.com/BabylonJS/Babylon.js), [retargeting](https://doc.babylonjs.com/features/featuresDeepDive/animation/animationRetargeting) | Credible alternate, not rejected on capability. Migration would replace the rendering integration without supplying real camera tracking automatically. |
| PlayCanvas | MIT engine with WebGL/WebGPU/WebXR, glTF and animation blend-tree examples. [Repository](https://github.com/playcanvas/engine), [examples](https://playcanvas.github.io/) | Credible alternate; hosted editor is not required for engine use. Same tracking caveat. |
| Rapier character controller | Built-in capsule move-and-slide, slopes, stairs and moving platform support. [Official documentation](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/character_controller/) | Select for subsequent authoritative 3D collision work when there is actual 3D collision geometry. Never claim a dog bounding box is a physical collider reconstructed in metres. |
| pmndrs Viverse / VRM starters | Ready third-person camera, humanoid motion/retargeting/BVH examples discovered. [Tutorial](https://pmndrs.github.io/viverse/tutorials/custom-character-controller), [starter](https://github.com/norio/vrm-game-starter) | Useful references. Requires React/VRM architecture adoption or additional license validation; no need to replace working authoritative world with a local-only controller. |
| Mixamo retarget helpers | Maintainer issue evidence includes root offset/sinking failures. [three-vrm issue](https://github.com/pixiv/three-vrm/issues/1643), [Three.js issue](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/25751) | Prefer matching authored rig/animations first. Do not introduce a retarget helper before testing bind poses, root motion and redistribution terms. |

Selection criteria: visible volumetric quality, free redistributable assets, matching rigs, independent multiplayer animation, compatibility with transparent OBS composition, low mobile cost, maintained documentation, and minimal disruption to server authority. No invented numerical benchmark or maintenance guarantee is used.

## Real spatial anchoring and occlusion

| Route | What primary sources actually establish | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native AR Foundation / ARCore / ARKit | AR Foundation acquires provider depth/stencil frames for occlusion; platform support differs. ARCore depth needs compatible devices and benefits from camera movement. [Unity occlusion](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.xr.arfoundation@6.1/manual/features/occlusion/occlusion-manager.html), [ARCore depth](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/depth) | Retain as preferred native mobile path. | WebXR immersive-ar | Standard gives an XR viewer pose and runtime composition. Basic AR module does not itself grant raw camera pixels or depth occlusion. | 8th Wall 2026 self-hosted engine | Hosted editor retired Feb 28, 2026; MIT framework excludes SLAM. Separate free unmodified binary contains SLAM. | 8th Wall binary restrictions | Section 1.2 restricts charged offerings whose value derives substantially from the binary, modification and competitive products. Original notices/license must remain. [Actual binary license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/8thwall/engine/main/LICENSE) | Do not silently bundle into commercial RaidRift. [Run](https://8thwall.org/docs/api/engine/xr8/run), [pipeline](https://8thwall.org/docs/api/engine/threejs/pipelinemodule) | Not a documented drop-in solver for arbitrary desktop OBS source footage. |
| Zappar / Mattercraft / Needle | Publisher documents mobile world-tracking integration with WebXR/native bridge routes. [Zappar Three.js](https://docs.zap.works/universal-ar/threejs/getting-started/), [Mattercraft](https://zap.works/mattercraft-for-8th-wall-studio-developers/), [Needle](https://engine.needle.tools/docs/tutorials/for-8thwall-users.html) | Alternatives needing exact deployment license/platform/capture validation; no subscription/signup authorized or created. |
| MindAR | MIT image/face tracking, explicitly not arbitrary markerless world SLAM. [Maintainer](https://github.com/hiukim/mind-ar-js) | Suitable optional image marker route, not a universal moving-world replacement. |
| AlvaAR / ORB-SLAM3 | AlvaAR runs visual SLAM in WASM and accepts video; GPLv3. ORB-SLAM3 is GPLv3, native and calibration-sensitive. [AlvaAR](https://github.com/alanross/AlvaAR), [ORB-SLAM3](https://github.com/UZ-SLAMLab/ORB_SLAM3) | Technically relevant desktop research path; not silently imported into the product with incompatible distribution assumptions. |
| Depth Anything V2 | Relative depth model family; Small weights Apache-2.0, Base/Large/Giant NC license. Maintainers link browser ONNX/Transformers.js examples. [Maintainer](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2), [Small model](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf) | Potential opt-in visual occlusion experiment. Relative depth is not camera pose, stable world scale or a metric mesh; temporal alignment and hardware cost need measurement. Do not ship NC weights. |

Inference from these interfaces: arbitrary stream video plus object rectangles cannot provide the same guarantees as calibrated pose/depth from a tracking runtime. A perspective virtual ground can improve visible volume immediately, but remains a declared scene model until actual pose/depth arrives.

Exa and Firecrawl connected tools were used. Parallel was checked in available tool inventory and Firecrawl provider discovery: no usable Parallel research tool/contract was available. [Parallel official search quickstart](https://docs.parallel.ai/search/search-quickstart) requires an API key. No credential exists here and no account/payment was created; therefore this report does not claim Parallel executed a search. Independent research branches were batched directly, without spawning agents.

Some retrievals failed: guessed KayKit Adventurers URL 404 (corrected above), guessed animation repo timeout, obsolete 8th Wall getting-started URL 404. Three.js anchor URLs initially returned navigation rather than the target page; direct pages were then read through their substantive content. Search excerpts were not treated as a validated download or functioning integration.

## Implementation sequence now authorized

1. Download/pin the official free KayKit archive; retain license, source commit and SHA256 manifest. Inspect real meshes, bones, animation clips and units. Fail visibly if loading fails; no silent substitution claimed as an imported asset.
2. Replace the named-character primitive rendering with independent skeletal actors, matching authored clips and bounded fallback poses for unsupported actions. Preserve own identity, portal arrival, equipment and authoritative commands.
3. Introduce perspective camera, coherent ground coordinates, depth sorting, light/shadow/contact and optional world-view orbit. Keep normalized image anchors aligned by ray/plane projection, clearly labelled as coarse desktop scene projection. Add actual model-ready diagnostics and projected bounds for QA.
4. Validate multiple independent characters, entrance, turning, wave/sit, near/far size, camera parallax, transparent output, portrait resizing, asset errors/disposal and real event integration. Inspect screenshots/video and measure actual cost before expanding.
5. Add capability-gated real tracking/pose route with actual supported runtime. Native build/device validation remains necessary; no world-lock or occlusion acceptance claim from a screenshot of characters over video.

Research phase completed before renderer implementation began. This decision is a starting point for real implementation/measurement, not a claim that the product is finished.
